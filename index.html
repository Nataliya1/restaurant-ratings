        About this map

        This map shows the current health inspection rating for restaurants, schools, and mobile food trucks in Douglas County, Nebraska.

        Ratings and inspection details come from the Douglas County Health Department's food establishment inspection program. Use the Filter by rating bar and the Restaurants / Schools / Mobile Food Trucks buttons above the map to search, browse, and download the underlying data.
