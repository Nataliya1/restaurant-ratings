This site shows current health inspection ratings for restaurants, schools, and mobile food trucks in Douglas County, Nebraska. Ratings and inspection details come from the Douglas County Health Department's food establishment inspection program.

Getting around this site

  • Interactive map — search by facility name or address, choose which facility types are shown, and select any point for its rating and inspection details.
  • See List — a full, searchable list of every restaurant, school, and mobile food truck, grouped by type, each with a Download CSV option. Mobile food trucks don't have a fixed location, so they're listed here rather than mapped.
  • Filter by rating — check or uncheck A, B, C, D, F, or Not rated / other to narrow results, on both the map and the facility list.
  • How ratings are assigned and FAQ — background on the inspection program and answers to common questions.
  • Language icon (in the header) — translate this site into one of 30 languages.
  • Contact us and Accessibility statement icons (also in the header) — for questions or accessibility needs.
